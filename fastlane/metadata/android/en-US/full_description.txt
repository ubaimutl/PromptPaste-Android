Plyph helps you correct, rewrite, transform, and run custom actions on selected text from Android apps.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Correct spelling, grammar, punctuation, and clarity</li>
<li>Rewrite selected text while preserving its meaning and tone</li>
<li>Create reusable custom actions with prompts and variables</li>
<li>Choose a provider, model, and token limits for individual actions</li>
<li>Review and edit generated text before replacing the selection</li>
<li>Use Android text-selection actions or the optional Plyph keyboard</li>
<li>Use a self-hosted Ollama server or supported cloud providers</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
<ul>
<li>No account is required</li>
<li>No analytics or telemetry</li>
<li>No accessibility service or background clipboard monitoring</li>
<li>Text is sent only after you explicitly run an action</li>
<li>API keys are stored locally and protected with Android Keystore</li>
</ul>

Cloud AI providers are third-party network services with their own privacy policies, retention rules, and usage limits. Plyph can instead connect to a self-hosted Ollama server.
